## 🛠️ Patterns & Problems

### 1. Two Pointers (Same Direction)
- Encode and Decode Strings
- Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II (but usually solve with Greedy)
- Merge Strings Alternately
- Rotate Array
- Merge Sorted Array
- Remove Duplicates From Sorted Array

### 2. Two Pointers (Opposite Direction)
- Remove Element
- Reverse String
- Valid Palindrome
- Valid Palindrome II
- Two Integer Sum II
- 3Sum
- 4Sum
- Container With Most Water
- Boats to Save People
- Trapping Rain Water

### 3. Three Pointers
- Sort Colors

---

## 🍀 Key Takeaways
- **Technique Type:** Iteration Strategy
- **Mental model:** Pointer Logic — Using two indices to reduce $O(n \times n)$ to $O(n)$.
